I am a Ph.D. student at [XLANG Lab](https://xlang.ai/), the University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. [Tao Yu](https://taoyds.github.io/).

I am now working on agentic foundation models, especially computer-use agent models ([OpenCUA](https://opencua.xlang.ai/)), agent evaluation ([Computer Agent Arena](https://arena.xlang.ai/), [OSWorld-Verified](https://xlang.ai/blog/osworld-verified)), and agent data synthesis ([Jedi](https://osworld-grounding.github.io/), [VideoAgentTrek](https://videoagenttrek.github.io/)). At UCSD, I worked on automatic LLM prompt optimization ([PromptAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16427)) and LLM Reasoning ([LLM Reasoners](https://www.llm-reasoners.net/)). I also worked in Prof. Zhuowen Tu's group, exploring how to improve diffusion models' conceptual performance.
