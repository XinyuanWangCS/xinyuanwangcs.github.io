I am a Ph.D. student at HKU, mentored by Prof. [Tao Yu](https://taoyds.github.io/). I obtained my master's degree from University of California, San Diego (UCSD). I was luckily to be mentored two distinguished professors at UCSD in Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision - Prof. [Zhiting Hu](http://zhiting.ucsd.edu/) and Prof. [Zhuowen Tu](https://pages.ucsd.edu/~ztu/). Prior to my study at UCSD, I graduated from Central South University (CSU) in Hunan, China, where I was mentored by Prof. [Ying Zhao](https://faculty.csu.edu.cn/zhaoying).

I am now working on agentic foundation models, especially computer-use agent models, including [Kimi K2.5](https://github.com/MoonshotAI/Kimi-K2.5), [OpenCUA](https://opencua.xlang.ai/) and [Kimi-VL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07491), agent evaluation ([Computer Agent Arena](https://arena.xlang.ai/), [OSWorld-Verified](https://xlang.ai/blog/osworld-verified)), and agent data synthesis ([Jedi](https://osworld-grounding.github.io/), [VideoAgentTrek](https://videoagenttrek.github.io/)). At UCSD, I worked on automatic LLM prompt optimization ([PromptAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16427)) and LLM Reasoning ([LLM Reasoners](https://www.llm-reasoners.net/)). I also worked in Prof. Zhuowen Tu's group, exploring how to improve diffusion models' conceptual performance.
